fix(checkpoint): collect writes at plain-value seed in delta channel history

Fixes langchain-ai/langgraph#8384

Reported by @Aarkin7, with two self-contained runnable repros and a search of the existing issues that turned up nothing. The report also pinned the shape of the bug precisely: the first write after migrating a thread to `DeltaChannel` is dropped, silently and permanently.

`InMemorySaver.get_delta_channel_history` skipped the writes stored at the ancestor it seeded from whenever that ancestor's blob was a plain value rather than a `_DeltaSnapshot`, silently dropping the first write made after migrating a thread to `DeltaChannel`.
